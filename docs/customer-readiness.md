# Customer readiness implementation and acceptance

The initial readiness delivery below is followed by the seven-phase visual/workflow implementation. The current decisions in this document supersede earlier correction, QA-request, manual-time and maintenance-handover behavior. Detection/model quality is a separate acceptance track. A working demo or a passing browser test does not certify a customer integration, a native binary, or store approval.

## Decisions implemented

- Browser remains the desktop app. Capacitor packages the same app for company-assigned individual iOS/Android devices.
- Offline preparation authorizes an organisation for 24 hours by default; administrator may choose 1–168 hours. Prepared visits include dossier/evidence/framework resources. Capture and existing review edits persist locally; detection, finalization and customer delivery need connectivity.
- New published template versions use resident-or-exception signing. Existing published templates retain their legacy contract for ordinary visits; reissued reports always require fresh signatures and a current revision. Publish a revised template before the pilot to enable the new policy. Signatures bind to the current dossier revision and are cleared when content changes.
- Agreements assign work to resident or corporation, with optional deadlines and evidence. No resident charges are calculated.
- Email sends the immutable PDF as an attachment. SMTP acceptance is not proof of inbox delivery. There is no automatic resend after an ambiguous post-DATA outcome.
- Follow-ups represent a new visit and carry structure and unresolved agreements. Editing an issued report reopens the same dossier and issues a new numbered report; previous snapshots, PDFs and signatures remain preserved. Audit events record changed field values. Legacy correction endpoints remain for compatibility but are removed from the inspector UI.
- Retention starts disabled. Save policy, review candidate counts, then explicitly activate. Report age begins at archival; raw audio and transcripts have separate terms. Holds, correction chains and active deliveries block applicable deletion. Backup retention remains operational policy.
- VERA is an internal ERP synchronization, without inspector approval. Administrators map and enable confirmed categories per supplier; raw audio and transcripts are separate explicit opt-ins. Read probes do not write. Sandbox write/read/cleanup tests require an explicitly configured sandbox. DICO chain-partner work ordering is deferred. Legacy maintenance-handover screens are removed from the active workflow; old receipts remain readable.

## Operator setup before the first customer visit

1. Configure the production origin/hosting, SMTP sender and verified integration hosts. Set `LEGAL_ENTITY`, `SUPPORT_EMAIL`, `PRIVACY_POLICY_URL`, `HOSTING_REGION` and the attachment limit. Production configuration rejects missing public operator details.
2. Import and validate the customer's property administration. Confirm source identity/object numbers; resolve provisional records before finalizing reports.
3. Publish the customer norm framework and pre/final templates. Approve evidence requirements and acknowledgement text. Configure report name/logo/tint and inspect a realistic PDF with long text, signatures, agreements and annotations.
4. Invite named users. Assign planner/reviewer/archival/purge privileges deliberately; existing custom roles do not silently gain new powers. Ensure at least two administrators before testing account deletion.
5. Prove the selected VERA categories and field mappings against the actual supplier sandbox. Attach read and separate write-test receipts, check evidence transfer, idempotent retries and error recovery. A working generic adapter is not supplier certification. Do not enable DICO maintenance ordering.
6. Agree retention, backup duration, support contact, incident ownership and tenant-exit process. Save retention disabled, review the preview and only activate after customer approval.
7. Follow the device/store release checklist. Run a complete realistic visit with a resident signature and with each exception, plus non-blocking reviewer feedback and editing/reissuing the same dossier.
8. Use administrator Analytics for automatically estimated capture/review/report activity and confirmed property issues. Idle/background time is excluded and overlapping devices do not double count a person. Historical manual records are not mixed into these estimates. Compare comparable visit kinds and sample sizes; these are activity estimates, not payroll hours or inspector rankings.

## Retention, exit and support limits

Deletion is asynchronous for object storage; inspect the durable deletion queue and retry failures. Previously downloaded or emailed copies belong to recipients and cannot be recalled. Minimal purge receipts retain actor, timestamp and stated reason, without the dossier itself. Final reports may retain derived descriptions after raw audio/transcripts expire.

Tenant exit is a ZIP in bounded parts (default 10, maximum 25 dossiers and 2 GiB per part), including configuration/catalog, report snapshots, exact PDF artifacts, source evidence, agreements, event and delivery history. Pause organisation writes, preserve the manifest `before` value and advance `after` using `lastInspectionId` until `hasMore` is false. Verify counts and open files before accepting an exit. Credentials and private authentication records are excluded. Missing evidence aborts a part; never accept a partial archive as successful.

Account deletion requires a fresh login (10 minutes), exact email confirmation and transfer of last-administrator ownership. It removes login identities/memberships and pseudonymizes the retained audit identity. Corporate dossiers follow organisation policy. Device cleanup refuses unsynced work. Historical report copies can contain the signer's name; the corporation must handle its retention obligations.

If the app reports local storage failure, keep the installed app and device, stop destructive troubleshooting, and contact the configured support contact. Do not reinstall/clear data while work exists locally. Resolve conflicts explicitly; never overwrite another inspector's evidence silently.

## External acceptance gates

The customer/supplier sandbox, production domain/hosting and developer accounts/Mac or macOS CI are being arranged by the owner. Their availability was assumed for preparation; no credentials, supplier acceptance receipt or signed store submission was supplied in this workspace. They remain release gates until evidence is attached. Android compilation passed locally in Docker; iOS compilation, native CI execution and physical-device acceptance must still be recorded independently of TypeScript/browser checks.
