DUTCH RESIDENTIAL INSPECTION SOURCE CATALOGUE
Checked: 30 October 2026 (UTC)

This is a source-discovery collection, not a labeled benchmark or training set.
It contains 20 records: 12 candidate sources, 4 acquisition leads, 2 unavailable
reports, 1 demonstration held for provenance review, and 1 fictional exclusion.
The 12 candidates comprise 10 directly accessible PDFs, 1 PDF readable through
the web research tool despite a direct HTTP 403, and 1 HTML excerpt collection.
No source is marked benchmark-ready or cleared for AI reuse.

FILES
source-catalogue.csv: spreadsheet-friendly ranked register, one row per source.
source-details.json: finding summaries and page locators, evidence/provenance,
  access checks, original-byte hashes, rights observations and limitations.

The CSV uses UTF-8 with a BOM for Excel and standard comma-separated quoting.
Physical PDF page positions are 1-based. Printed page labels may differ.
Dates refer to report/inspection dates where stated; lead publication dates
are distinguished in the JSON. Access status reflects this check, not a promise
of future availability. No accounts, payments or third-party messages were used.

WHERE TO START
Ranks 1-3: new-build handover photographs and two roof/envelope reports with
explicit finding-to-image links. Ranks 4-6 add whole-dwelling component coverage.
The remaining candidates offer low-resolution photos, comparison conditions,
mixed report excerpts, access limitations or conflicting report summaries.
Two reports from one provider are not two independent provider sources.
Coverage is strongest for roofs, facades, timber and finishing. Mould evidence
is limited to partial excerpts and a VvE report with conflicting summaries;
this collection does not establish balanced defect or room coverage.

CORPORATION COVERAGE
No accessible corporation-owned report corpus was confirmed. The indexed
Woonstad report and resident-produced Rochdale complaint inventory both return
404. The Ressort Wonen customer case is a concrete acquisition lead. The Dutch
NHL Stenden/AeroScan research and the commercial defect library are also leads,
not downloaded image datasets. Other residential reports are clearly marked.

READING THE CATEGORIES
candidate_report: photos and findings checked on selected pages; later
  provenance, rights and expert review are still needed.
candidate_report_web_access_only: text accessible through the web research tool;
  normal retrieval and image pairing remain unresolved.
candidate_report_excerpt: partial published report images; no full PDF verified.
candidate_comparison_report: potential sound-condition examples, not negatives.
template_hold: demonstration whose real-inspection provenance is unconfirmed.
excluded_fictional: findings explicitly fictional; exclude as real ground truth.
unavailable_report: original inaccessible, even if search engines index its text.
acquisition_lead: data holder/library identified; no usable public corpus verified.

REUSE AND EVIDENCE LIMITS
Public access was checked separately from permission to reuse. Some reports
explicitly reserve rights or restrict copying. A missing license statement does
not establish permission. No report was treated as legally cleared.

Report findings are source statements, not labels created in this task. Some
reports have inconsistent summaries, component scores or location headings.
General boilerplate does not prove a defect exists. A photo cannot establish
every cause, material diagnosis, measurement, functional test or responsibility.
Do not equate suspected asbestos with a confirmed material classification.
Do not equate no finding with a defect-free photo. Report photos can illustrate
only part of a paragraph's claims. RGB and thermal evidence must stay distinct.

FUTURE PREPARATION (NOT PERFORMED)
Establish rights/provenance, obtain adequate source resolution, match each photo
to its actual observation, and have an inspector review the intended labels.
Exclude covers, signatures, stock images and unrelated diagrams; account for
personal details before distributing derivatives. Deduplicate and group by
property/source before making train/test splits. Public samples may already
have been seen by a model, so a future private holdout is needed for stronger
generalization claims.

Only catalogue metadata is delivered. Original PDFs/images were read in memory;
no raw reports, image crops, annotations, model evaluations or app changes are
included. The shortlist is deliberately not represented as a valid benchmark.
