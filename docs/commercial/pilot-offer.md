# Satvey Inspect pilot proposal

Draft for a housing corporation managing approximately 10,000 homes. The pilot evaluates whether Satvey reduces inspection administration while preserving the corporation's required inspection quality.

## Proposed pilot

An eight-week pilot costs EUR 5,000 excluding VAT and covers up to five inspectors and 250 finalized preliminary or final inspections. It includes one property CSV import, configuration of one corporation-approved quality framework, preliminary and final inspection formats, and two training sessions.

Inspectors capture photos and voice notes, review generated proposals and quality assessments, and finalize signed inspection reports. The inspector approves the final content. A named corporation quality lead verifies samples and resolves policy questions.

## Schedule and evaluation

Before live use, agree the quality framework, device/connectivity checks and baseline measurement with the maintenance manager, quality lead and IT contact. Compare representative inspections with the current workflow, including review, corrections, report preparation and downstream re-entry.

The target is at least 20 minutes net saving per inspection, equivalent or better inspection quality, and successful use in normal field conditions. The parties review adoption, source-data correctness, recovery after connectivity loss and support effort as well as the time result. The evaluation follows the [pilot measurement protocol](pilot-measurement.md).

## Annual offer after the pilot

The reference annual subscription is EUR 12,000 excluding VAT for 1,500 finalized inspections, ten editing users, unlimited internal read-only users, existing AI processing, formats, frameworks, reporting, SSO and standard support. Additional capacity can be agreed at EUR 4 per finalized inspection. Drafts, corrections and repeated report exports do not use extra capacity.

The pilot's EUR 5,000 payment covers standard implementation on conversion; it is not charged again. Supplier-specific integrations, additional sources and bespoke configuration require a separate scope and price. The annual subscription starts on the agreed production start date, after explicit acceptance, and is a separate commitment from the pilot.

## Optional maintenance BI discovery

A future Maintenance BI module would combine inspection data with work orders, maintenance costs and contractor results. The reference annual budget is EUR 15,000 in addition to Inspect, with EUR 10,000 to EUR 20,000 implementation subject to source access, data history and mapping. This is a planning budget for separately agreed future scope and is not included in the inspection pilot.

Before quoting delivery, identify two recurring maintenance decisions, the responsible managers, existing BI capabilities and the data needed to verify the result. A scoped BI pilot must establish agreed KPIs, reconcile samples to source records and measure reporting effort and decision outcomes separately. Power BI licences or capacity, if used, must be specified in the scoped quote.

## Items to complete for a customer offer

Fill in the corporation name, named contacts, actual annual inspection volume, planned start date and the applicable service, data-processing, retention and exit terms before issuing a customer-specific offer. Confirm hosting and supplier access with IT. No part of this draft promises maintenance optimization, resident messaging or complete offline reopening in the current inspection product.
