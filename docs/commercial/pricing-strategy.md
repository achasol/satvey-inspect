# Satvey pricing and market analysis

For a reference corporation with 10,000 homes, propose EUR 12,000 a year for Inspect and EUR 5,000 for standard onboarding. For a future maintenance BI product that combines inspection, work-order, cost and contractor data, test an additional EUR 12,000 to EUR 20,000 a year. An opening offer of EUR 27,000 a year for Inspect plus BI consists of EUR 12,000 for Inspect and EUR 15,000 for BI.

These prices are commercial hypotheses to test with first customers. They assume Satvey works alongside the existing ERP and CRM. All Satvey prices exclude VAT. Research date: 30 October 2026.

## Current inspection value

Satvey supports photo and voice capture, hosted transcription, AI finding proposals, corporation-specific quality frameworks, published inspection formats, explicit human review, frozen signed reports, property imports, a VERA property reader, role management and OIDC SSO. See [product behavior](../development.md).

The strongest case for a premium is faster capture combined with consistent application of the corporation's own policy. Measure total active inspection-to-report time, including corrections, review and downstream re-entry. Basic report generation and signatures face inexpensive alternatives.

Maintenance optimization and resident communication are future scope. The current VERA reader does not write maintenance results back to the ERP; supplier-specific integration needs validation. The inspection quality framework does not calculate NEN 2767 condition scores. Local draft recovery does not guarantee reopening the app fully offline. Repository features do not establish customer savings or production performance.

## Inspection packaging

| Reference portfolio | Assumed annual inspections | Annual price guidance    |
| ------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| 5,000 homes         | 700                        | EUR 8,000 to EUR 10,000  |
| 10,000 homes        | 1,400                      | EUR 10,000 to EUR 15,000 |
| 15,000 homes        | 2,100                      | EUR 14,000 to EUR 20,000 |

The reference volume assumes 7% annual turnover and two inspections per move. BrabantWonen reports a 6.9% sector turnover figure for 2024 in its [2025 benchmark explanation](https://www.brabantwonen.nl/actueel/2025/11/20/aedes-benchmark-2025-bekend). Replace this assumption with actual completed preliminary and final inspections; a home, tenancy change and report are different billing units.

The opening EUR 12,000 annual package includes 1,500 finalized inspections, ten editing users, unlimited internal read-only users and standard support. Include existing AI processing, frameworks, formats, reports and SSO. Do not charge for draft work, corrections, export retries or repeated exports of the same inspection. Price additional agreed report capacity at EUR 4 per inspection and agree extensions before the allowance is exceeded.

Standard implementation is EUR 5,000 for one property CSV source, one quality framework, two inspection formats and two training sessions. Scope supplier-specific integrations separately. Offer an eight-week EUR 5,000 pilot; on conversion that payment satisfies the standard EUR 5,000 implementation fee. It does not reduce the annual subscription or trigger another onboarding charge.

At 1,400 annual inspections and an assumed EUR 65 hourly staff cost, net savings of 10, 20, 25 and 30 minutes produce annual capacity values of EUR 15,167, EUR 30,333, EUR 37,917 and EUR 45,500 respectively. The EUR 12,000 subscription breaks even at about eight minutes saved. First-year costs of EUR 12,000 subscription, EUR 5,000 implementation and an illustrative EUR 3,000 customer adoption effort require approximately 13 minutes saved. Capacity value becomes cash savings only when an actual expense falls.

## Maintenance BI packaging

| Package                      | Capability required                                                                                                                                              | Incremental annual guidance for 10,000 homes                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inspection reporting         | Inspection volume, completeness, defects and quality trends using Satvey data                                                                                    | Include a useful standard set; test EUR 2,000 to EUR 5,000 for advanced reporting if it solves an additional recurring task.                         |
| Maintenance BI               | Managed connections to work orders, actual costs, property and contractor data; agreed KPI definitions; historical trends; drill-through and scheduled reporting | EUR 12,000 to EUR 20,000; opening target EUR 15,000.                                                                                                 |
| Maintenance decision support | Explainable forecasts and scenarios, repair/replace comparisons and prioritization tied to approved actions and measured outcomes                                | EUR 20,000 to EUR 35,000 for the BI module in total, replacing the Maintenance BI fee. Sell only with validated capabilities and additional benefit. |

For Maintenance BI, propose EUR 10,000 to EUR 20,000 implementation after scoping available sources, access, history, mapping and reconciliation. Standardize repeatable connectors and charge for extra sources, custom dashboards and material model changes separately. Separate any Microsoft licences or embedded capacity costs explicitly; unlimited Satvey viewers do not establish Power BI licensing rights.

The EUR 27,000 annual opening offer is EUR 12,000 Inspect plus EUR 15,000 Maintenance BI. Standard Inspect implementation is EUR 5,000 and the BI implementation budget begins at EUR 10,000, giving a reference first-year supplier total of EUR 42,000 before custom integrations and customer effort. A previous inspection pilot payment counts toward that EUR 5,000, never on top of it.

Microsoft lists [Power BI Pro at EUR 12.10 and Premium Per User at EUR 20.80 per user per month](https://www.microsoft.com/nl-nl/power-platform/products/power-bi/pricing), paid annually and excluding VAT. Twenty Pro licences therefore cost EUR 2,904 a year, before data work, implementation and operations. Some buyers already have Pro through E5. Embedded capacity has a different pricing model. Satvey's premium must pay for dependable maintenance data, sector definitions, managed integrations and recurring decisions.

[Zig Insights](https://www.zig.eu/nl/zig365/insights/dashboards/) already describes dashboards for daily maintenance, planned maintenance, actual costs, contractors, lead times and budgets. [PCA's Dynhus case](https://pca.nl/blogartikelen/klantverhalen/dynhus-digitaliseren-met-aandacht-voor-de-mens/) describes BI for travel time, first-time fixes and unsuccessful jobs. Neither reviewed page supplied a BI list price. Validate differentiation against the customer's current reporting and its actual incremental cost.

Maintenance BI should answer practical questions: which complexes have repeat failures, which comparable contractor jobs have unusual cost or revisit rates, where spending exceeds commitments and budgets, and which approved interventions changed outcomes. Normalize contractor comparisons for job type and property complexity. Distinguish missing data from zero performance and show refresh dates, denominators and source records.

### BI business case

For illustration only, assume EUR 10 million of addressable annual maintenance expenditure and a BI fee of EUR 15,000. An additional 0.5% realized saving attributable to decisions supported by BI is EUR 50,000. Ten hours of separate reporting work saved each month at EUR 65 an hour adds EUR 7,800, for EUR 57,800 of annual benefit. Do not count a reporting task again within inspection savings.

On expenditure alone, the fee breaks even at 0.15% attributable saving; a three-times annual benefit-to-fee target requires 0.45%. These percentages are arithmetic thresholds, not expected product performance. Use only expenditure genuinely influenced by the proposed decisions. Postponed essential work, underspending against an inflated budget and unrealized recommendations are not cash savings. Add implementation, customer effort and the cost of interventions when evaluating first-year economics.

## Future suite packaging

The future suite connects inspection evidence to approved maintenance needs, timing and assignment, resident updates and completion evidence. The ERP retains core administration and financial records; existing CRM/portal channels can carry resident communications.

For a proven 10,000-home deployment, retain the earlier EUR 35,000 to EUR 60,000 annual target for inspection, maintenance timing, workforce scheduling and resident communication. With integrated Maintenance BI, test a combined EUR 45,000 to EUR 75,000 annual range, with implementation separate. These are alternative bundle totals, not additional fees on top of each other. An opening bundle target is EUR 55,000 a year, approximately EUR 0.46 per home per month.

Do not add module prices mechanically: planning and BI may contribute to the same avoided repair or staffing improvement. At EUR 55,000, target at least EUR 165,000 of distinct, recurring customer benefit as a commercial acceptance goal. Reserve a EUR 75,000-plus discussion for wider deployments with greater scope and evidenced value, rather than the first pilot.

Budget owners are likely the maintenance manager and property/asset director, with controller and BI/IT participation. Confirm who owns the source access, KPI definitions and financial acceptance before scoping a BI pilot.

Aim for at least 75% recurring gross margin after direct hosting, inference, storage, connector operations, vendor licences and support. At EUR 12,000 Inspect that leaves EUR 3,000 a year for direct delivery; at EUR 15,000 BI it leaves EUR 3,750. Measure actual costs and keep bespoke implementation outside recurring standard support.
