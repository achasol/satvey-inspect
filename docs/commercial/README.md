# Satvey commercial materials

For the first paid pilots with Dutch housing corporations, offer Satvey Inspect at EUR 12,000 a year plus EUR 5,000 implementation for a reference customer with 10,000 homes and approximately 1,400 inspections a year. A future maintenance BI module should have its own scope, implementation fee and measured business case.

All Satvey prices below exclude VAT. These are proposed commercial terms, not existing product entitlements or commitments to customers. Research date: 30 October 2026.

| [Pilot proposal](pilot-offer.md)                   | Customer-facing draft for an inspection pilot                                                                 |

The BI and scheduling packages describe future scope. Before quoting them, confirm that the required capabilities and source-system integrations can be delivered. The calculator's example savings are assumptions and can be replaced with customer evidence. It runs locally without sending inputs to a server.
