Inspection assumptions

The reference volume is 10,000 homes × 7% annual turnover × two inspections per move. Enter the customer's actual eligible inspections. Changing homes does not automatically change volume or quoted fees.

Maintenance BI assumptions

Use savings after intervention costs and exclude outcomes counted for inspection, planning or other products. A deferred essential repair is not a saving. The illustrative preset assumes 0.5%; this is not a forecast or observed Satvey result.

Formulas and evidence

Inspection value = annual inspections × minutes saved ÷ 60 × staff hourly cost. Reporting value = monthly reporting hours saved × 12 × reporting hourly cost. Maintenance value = addressable annual expenditure × net attributable saving percentage ÷ 100. All three benefits must be distinct.

Recurring cost = selected module fees + annual vendor costs + annual internal effort. First-year cost adds supplier implementation and one-time customer effort. Benefit-to-cost = annual benefit ÷ cost. Net return = (annual benefit − cost) ÷ cost. Zero denominators show “Not defined”.

Microsoft lists Power BI Pro at EUR 12.10 per user per month, billed annually excluding VAT; the visualization licence alone excludes source integration and managed maintenance analytics. Zig Insights already describes maintenance dashboards and drill-through.
