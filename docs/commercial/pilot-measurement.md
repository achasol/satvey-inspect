# Satvey pilot measurement

Measure incremental improvement over the corporation's current workflow, using separate records for inspection effort, reporting effort and actual maintenance decisions. Every claimed benefit needs a baseline, accountable customer owner, observation period and calculation.

## Inspection baseline and comparison

The customer maintenance manager owns acceptance and the quality lead owns independent review. In weeks one and two, record at least 30 representative baseline inspections if volume permits. Use weeks three to six for normal Satvey use after training, targeting at least 60 completed inspections. Use weeks seven and eight to reconcile results and agree the annual case. If volumes are insufficient, report the sample size and extend measurement before treating the result as representative.

For every inspection, record the anonymized inspection reference, preliminary/final type, complexity, inspector, device/connectivity context, active capture minutes, review/correction minutes, report minutes and downstream re-entry minutes. Keep elapsed waiting time separate from active effort and record interruptions consistently. Do not copy resident names, addresses, media or credentials into the commercial calculator.

Compare similar type/complexity groups and show both mean and median time, sample sizes and the distribution of savings across inspectors. Include all attempted pilot inspections and record failed, abandoned and manually completed cases. Do not remove difficult cases after seeing their outcome.

Have the quality lead review at least 20 baseline and 20 pilot dossiers, preferably without knowing which workflow produced each dossier. Use the same rubric: required evidence and answers present, findings supported by evidence, correct policy application and usable final report. Investigate critical missed findings individually. Treat any material quality regression as a reason to fix and repeat the affected measurement before conversion.

## Acceptance and operational scenarios

The commercial target is at least 20 minutes mean net active saving per inspection with equivalent or better quality. Review the median and individual results to identify whether a few cases dominate the mean. Confirm normal users can finish the process, with reviewed evidence, without ongoing supplier intervention.

Verify poor connectivity during capture, interrupted upload, device recovery, correction of an AI proposal, missing evidence, manual continuation after provider failure, final signatures and PDF export. Validate SSO and actual supplier integration if included in the agreed rollout. Test reopening offline explicitly; do not interpret saved local drafts as a guarantee of that capability.

Record weekly active inspectors, completed versus attempted inspections, support hours, rework and any downstream duplicate entry. Annualize using the customer's actual eligible volume and active staff cost. Include adoption and implementation effort. A saved hour is capacity value unless the customer identifies an actual expense reduction.

## BI discovery and acceptance

Before starting a separately scoped BI pilot, choose two recurring decisions, such as repeat repairs by complex and unusual contractor revisit rates. Identify the maintenance owner, controller, data/BI contact and relevant ERP, work-order, cost and contractor sources. Establish which decisions and reports the existing BI system already supports.

Agree KPI definitions, source keys, maintenance type, comparable job categories, time periods, exclusions, denominator and refresh interval. Reconcile costs with the agreed source ledger or work-order totals and investigate differences. Verify that duplicates, credits, reopened jobs and canceled work are treated consistently. Display missing/stale data and distinguish it from a zero result.

Test portfolio/complex/contractor filters, drill-through to permitted source records, historical comparisons, scheduled report delivery and permissions. A manager must be able to trace a material result to its underlying data. Avoid ranking contractors across different work types without adjustment.

The BI pilot succeeds when the agreed sources refresh reliably within the agreed interval, material totals reconcile with the controller-approved source, the two decisions can be completed with traceable evidence, and the measured incremental annual benefit supports the proposed fee. Use at least three times recurring fee as a target, not a claimed result. Financial outcomes requiring longer than the pilot remain unvalidated until observed.

## Benefit ledger and calculations

Maintain a ledger with benefit ID, affected process, baseline, changed decision, implementation date, owner, evidence, gross benefit, action costs, net benefit and overlap with other modules. Assign each outcome to one benefit record. Planning, inspection and BI can share attribution within that record; the total cannot exceed the actual outcome.

| Measure                                | Calculation                                                                                                                               |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Annual inspection capacity value       | Eligible annual inspections multiplied by net minutes saved divided by 60, multiplied by active hourly staff cost                         |
| Annual reporting capacity value        | Distinct reporting hours saved per month multiplied by 12, multiplied by reporting staff hourly cost                                      |
| Annual attributable maintenance saving | Addressable annual expenditure multiplied by the evidenced incremental saving rate, net of intervention costs and quality/service effects |
| Recurring customer cost                | Annual software fees plus vendor licences/capacity plus recurring customer administration effort                                          |
| First-year customer cost               | Recurring cost plus supplier implementation plus customer implementation/adoption effort                                                  |
| Benefit to cost                        | Distinct annual benefit divided by the relevant cost; undefined when cost is zero                                                         |
| Net return                             | Distinct annual benefit minus the relevant cost, divided by that cost; show a negative return when costs exceed benefits                  |

The calculator accepts a net attributable maintenance saving rate. It must not include savings counted in another module, deferred essential maintenance or intervention costs omitted from the net result. Use zero until a customer can defend an assumption or measurement, and label forecast cases as assumptions.

## Commercial decision

At pilot close, show baseline versus pilot results, sample sizes, quality review, unresolved field issues, direct delivery costs and the financial case. The maintenance manager accepts operations, the quality lead accepts inspection quality, the controller accepts benefit calculations and IT confirms the scoped integrations. A pilot does not automatically become an annual contract.
